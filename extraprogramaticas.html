Extracurricular Programs

Experiences with families and children.

We love these activities. Together as families, we take part in experiences connected to the environment, nature, culture, music, art and reading. Children join these gatherings, which aim to enrich their childhood through meaningful, shared moments.

Until now, however, these activities have depended entirely on volunteers, which makes it hard to run them in a systematic and consistent way. We urgently need initiatives that are here to stay: programs that genuinely help develop skills linked to the natural environment, sport, crafts, carpentry and other creative or practical fields.

We dream of building an outdoor school, where learning continues beyond the classroom. A place where young people know they have somewhere meaningful to go after school, somewhere to learn, create and explore, rather than losing the energy and curiosity so characteristic of childhood.

We want motivated young people with opportunities to discover their talents, express their creativity and channel their energy into projects that connect them with their land, their community and their future.
