Music Trail

Mobile Library

The Music Trail is a travelling education program that brings music learning to the rural schools of Puerto Guadal, Mallín Grande and Puerto Bertrand.

Our aim is to spark students' interest in music and their feel for it as part of everyday life, through workshops built on play, movement and learning to play an instrument. Along the way, we encourage their creativity, their artistic expression and their social and emotional well-being.
