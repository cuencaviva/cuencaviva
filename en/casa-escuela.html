Casa Escuela

Secondary education for young people around the lake.

Casa Escuela was created so that young people from Puerto Guadal and the communities around the lake can get a good secondary education without having to leave their community and their surroundings.

An integrated education rooted in its setting

A rounded education: three dimensions brought together in one experience.

1

Academic

Learning based on the national curriculum, with personal support and technology that serves learning.

2

Technical and practical

Learning by doing, through projects, trades, experiences and activities that let students explore their interests and build practical skills.

3

Personal growth and values

Growing as people: learning to know themselves, relate to others and take an active part in the world around them.

Technology that serves learning

A tool that makes good education possible in rural communities.

Technology gives students access to high-quality content and learning resources, lets each of them learn at their own pace, and makes it possible to follow every student's progress.

Learning from this place, growing towards the world

We see where we live as an opportunity for learning, not a limitation. Its nature, its places, its communities and the knowledge of the people who live here are a source of experience and understanding that enriches education.

Projects, hands-on activities, outings and real experiences let students learn from their own reality and in dialogue with the world around them.

Opening paths to the future

Casa Escuela wants young people to broaden their horizons, discover their interests and build their own path, without losing sight of where they started.

We want to give them the tools and skills to go on to higher education, or to follow a technical and practical path linked to the opportunities in our region, with a focus on tourism, conservation and the work they can offer.

Because staying can also be a way to grow.
