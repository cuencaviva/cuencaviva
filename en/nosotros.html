About us

Who we are, what we're working towards and the people behind it.

Who we are

Fundación Cuenca Viva was started by the families and communities around Lago General Carrera to widen the educational, cultural and learning opportunities open to the children and young people growing up here.

We work with the school communities of Puerto Guadal, Puerto Bertrand and Mallín Grande, building networks and bringing initiatives together so that new experiences reach the area and last.

Our mission

To connect children and young people with the learning opportunities that exist inside and outside school, treating this place as a unique source of experience, knowledge and possibility.

We work with schools, families, organisations and local people so children can explore, create, discover and broaden their horizons, drawing on what their own community knows and on new opportunities that connect their home with the wider world.

Growing up somewhere rural and remote shouldn't limit what a child can learn or become.
