After-school Activities

Experiences for children and families.

These are some of our favourite activities. Families come together for outings and sessions on nature, culture, music, art and reading, and children get shared experiences they remember.

Until now they have run entirely on volunteer time, which makes it hard to hold them regularly. We need programs that last: ones that teach skills linked to the outdoors, sport, crafts, carpentry and other practical and creative work.

We want young people to have the chance to find their talents and put their energy into projects that connect them to their land, their community and their future.
