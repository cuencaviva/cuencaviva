New School Library

A place to discover the joy of reading.

In 2025, with support from Fundación Angelini, we renewed the school library in Puerto Guadal. It now has a fresh collection of up-to-date books and a space designed to draw children in.

Fundación La Fuente and Corporación Troquel helped us choose the books, so every child can find something right for their age and interests.

The library works alongside the Reading Route, which takes the Bibliomóvil to schools all around the lake.
