Who we are

We support the children of three rural schools around Lago General Carrera in Chilean Patagonia: Puerto Guadal, Puerto Bertrand and Mallín Grande. Working with teachers and parents, we run reading, music, character and outdoor programs that a remote school would struggle to offer on its own.

A child's education doesn't stop at the classroom door, so we work with families and the wider community too. That shared effort is what keeps projects going year after year.

There is a lot to learn from this place. National experts run successful horticulture projects in the area, the rivers and hills hold fossils waiting to be found, and people working in conservation and sustainable development are right on our doorstep. We want our students to learn from all of it.
