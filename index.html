Our commitment to education

We work to support children's education in the General Carrera Lake basin. Our focus is on assisting learning processes, contributing experiences, and developing educational programs in partnership with schools and parents.

Our aim is not only to expand educational opportunities, but also to strengthen a lasting commitment to the growth and well-being of entire school communities. Unity and good coordination between families and the wider community are essential for the sustainability and development of educational initiatives in the region.

Today there are many opportunities to implement learning initiatives that can significantly improve children's quality of life and future potential. These range from horticulture workshops taught by leading national experts who have built successful projects in this abundant and still undeveloped region, to outdoor educational programs that draw on the remarkable environment around us. From discovering fossils hidden in rivers and mountains to learning about sustainable development and conservation from those who work directly in the field, we have the chance to offer a first-class educational experience.
